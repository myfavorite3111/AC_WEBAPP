# Demo Navigation Flow for 60-90 Second Trailer

Recommended account: `demo_ceo` / `Demo@12345`

Use smooth cuts and avoid pages that require long form entry.

## 1. Public Demo Start

Page: `/`
Action: Show the public demo landing screen and the two demo roles.
Record to open: none.
Caption: Complete AC service business management.

## 2. Login

Page: `/login/`
Action: Click `CEO / Owner` under Use Demo Account, then click `Login to ERP`.
Record to open: demo CEO account.
Caption: Secure owner and manager access.

## 3. Business Dashboard

Page: `/dashboard/`
Action: Slowly pan across dashboard cards and insurance/service alert area.
Record to open: none.
Caption: See your business overview in one place.

## 4. Customer Management

Page: `/customers/`
Action: Search or visually highlight `Northstar Business Park`, then open its detail page.
Record to open: `Northstar Business Park`.
Caption: Manage residential, commercial, warranty, and AMC customers.

## 5. Service Requests

Page: `/service/`
Action: Open `SER0003 - Northstar Business Park` or the first visible commercial complaint.
Record to open: `Server room cassette AC water leakage`.
Caption: Track every repair and maintenance request.

## 6. Service Schedule / Reminders

Page: `/customers/service-schedules/`
Action: Show pending AMC/warranty services first; avoid editing during the trailer.
Record to open: any pending `AMC Service` for `Green Valley Residency` or `Northstar Business Park`.
Caption: Never miss scheduled service visits.

## 7. Projects

Page: `/projects/`
Action: Open `VRV retrofit - Block A`.
Record to open: Project `PRJ0001` / `VRV retrofit - Block A`.
Caption: Control installation projects from planning to commissioning.

## 8. BOQ / Quotation Story

Page: `/boq/`
Action: Open `VRV system BOQ` for the Northstar project.
Record to open: `BOQ0001 - VRV system BOQ`.
Caption: Plan material and quotation details clearly.

## 9. Material Issue

Page: `/material-issue/`
Action: Open `Material for VRV riser work`.
Record to open: `MIS0001`.
Caption: Issue material to technicians and sites with stock control.

## 10. Store and Spare Parts

Page: `/store/items/`
Action: Show AC units, copper pipes, gas, controls, and service spares.
Record to open: `Copper Pipe - 1/2 inch` or `VRV Indoor Unit - 2 HP`.
Caption: Manage AC units, gas, copper pipe, and spare parts.

## 11. Store Transactions

Page: `/store/transactions/`
Action: Show Stock In, Stock Out, Project Work, Purchase, and Service Work rows.
Record to open: first visible transaction.
Caption: Track every stock movement.

## 12. AMC Records

Page: `/amc/`
Action: Open `Northstar Business Park` AMC contract.
Record to open: Northstar active AMC contract.
Caption: Organize AMC contracts and technician visits.

## 13. Reports

Page: `/reports/`
Action: Show report cards, then briefly open project consumption or customer service history report.
Record to open: Project consumption report for `VRV retrofit - Block A` if available.
Caption: Better reports, better control.

## 14. Closing Shot

Page: `/dashboard/`
Action: Return to dashboard.
Record to open: none.
Caption: Reduce manual work. Run your AC service business from one system.
