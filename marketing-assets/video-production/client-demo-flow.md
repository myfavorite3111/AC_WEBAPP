# Client Demo Flow

1. Complete business overview - `/dashboard/`
2. Manage all customers in one place - `/customers/`
3. Open full customer details - `/customers/detail/51/`
4. Maintain complete service history - `/complaints/customer-history/`
5. Track service schedules - `/customers/service-schedules/`
6. Monitor repair job status - `/service/`
7. Review a service request - `/service/detail/27/`
8. Manage installation projects - `/projects/`
9. Open commercial project details - `/projects/detail/25/`
10. Prepare BOQs and quotations - `/boq/detail/21/`
11. Track AMC contracts - `/amc/`
12. Review AMC visit planning - `/amc/detail/22/`
13. Monitor inventory and spare parts - `/store/items/`
14. View stock movement records - `/store/transactions/`
15. Record material issued to teams - `/material-issue/detail/17/`
16. Review reports and business control - `/reports/`
