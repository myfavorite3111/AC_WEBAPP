# Air Conditioning Services ERP

## What This Software Does

Air Conditioning Services ERP helps AC service and installation businesses manage daily work from one place. It keeps customer details, service requests, AMC visits, installation projects, spare parts, material issue, and reports organized.

It is useful for businesses that want to reduce paperwork, avoid missed follow-ups, track technician work, and manage customers more professionally.

## Problems It Helps Solve

- Too much manual paperwork
- Missed service and AMC follow-ups
- Customer records spread across notebooks, Excel, and WhatsApp
- Difficulty tracking pending and completed jobs
- Confusion in spare parts and stock records
- Delays in AMC visit planning
- Unorganized BOQ, quotation, and project records

## Main Features

| Feature | Business Benefit |

## Simple Example

A customer calls for AC repair or installation. The team records the customer and job, schedules the work, tracks the technician or team, issues required material from stock, updates the job status, and keeps the service history for future reference.

The owner can review customers, pending work, AMC visits, stock, projects, and reports from the dashboard.
