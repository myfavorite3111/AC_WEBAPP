# 60-90 Second Trailer Scene Plan

Opening title: Air Conditioning Services ERP - Complete AC Business Management

1. Dashboard overview (0:00-0:08)
On-screen text: See your complete AC service business in one place.
Show: Main dashboard cards, alerts, and key totals.
Action: Slow pan over dashboard metrics.
Transition: Quick fade.

2. Customers and properties (0:08-0:17)
On-screen text: Manage residential, commercial, warranty, and AMC customers.
Show: Customer list, customer profile, service history.
Action: Search a customer, open profile.
Transition: Slide left.

3. Service requests (0:17-0:27)
On-screen text: Track every installation, repair, and maintenance request.
Show: Complaint/service list with pending, in progress, completed statuses.
Action: Open a service request and point to technician/status.
Transition: Zoom cut.

4. Projects and BOQ (0:27-0:39)
On-screen text: Plan AC projects with BOQ and material control.
Show: Project list, project detail, BOQ detail.
Action: Open a commercial project and show material quantities.
Transition: Fast dissolve.

5. Technician assignment and schedule (0:39-0:51)
On-screen text: Assign teams and never miss scheduled service visits.
Show: AMC/service schedule with pending items first.
Action: Mark one service as complete.
Transition: Swipe.

6. Store and spare parts (0:51-1:03)
On-screen text: Control AC units, copper pipe, gas, spares, and stock movement.
Show: Store dashboard, item list, transaction list.
Action: Search an item, show stock in/out transaction.
Transition: Fade.

7. Quotations, invoices, and payments story (1:03-1:15)
On-screen text: Keep quotations, BOQs, material issues, and billing records organized.
Show: BOQ report, material issue print/PDF, project value fields.
Action: Open report/download view.
Transition: Clean cut.

8. Reports and control (1:15-1:25)
On-screen text: Make faster decisions with live reports and service history.
Show: Reports dashboard, consumption report, customer service history.
Action: Scroll through report cards.
Transition: Fade to title.

Closing call to action (1:25-1:30)
On-screen text: Run your AC service business from one system. Book a demo today.
Show: Login or dashboard hero view.
